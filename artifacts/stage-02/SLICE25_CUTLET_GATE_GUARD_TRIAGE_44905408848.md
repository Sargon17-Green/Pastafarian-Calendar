## Correctness-preserving hypothesis and engineering gate

In accepted production `sql/20_stage02_cutlet_partition.sql` at blob `42acfb80e9eabfd9d091176643763ee7d7bca9ee`, `gate_at_day := exact_gate_index(p_calculation_day)` is always evaluated. But the result influences `required_cut` only if it is a gate index **strictly between** `p_year.open_gate_index` and `p_year.close_gate_index`.

The year gate sequence has strictly increasing days. Therefore, if `calculation_day <= open_gate_day` or `calculation_day >= close_gate_day`, the calculation day cannot be an interior gate and `required_cut` must remain NULL. The QA candidate adds an explicit day-range guard **before** the expensive lookup; interior dates preserve the unmodified lookup and index boundary test.

- Local independent model: **25,000/25,000** cases with independently enumerated strictly increasing internal gate days, including before/open/inside/close/after boundaries, random positive and negative indices; **12,617/25,000** lookup calls bypassed in the synthetic model. This is arithmetic/static evidence, not native performance or equivalence proof for all legal DB states.
