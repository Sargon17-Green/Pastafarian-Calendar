For nonnegative canonical limbs, compute c+a*b by output diagonal k. Initialize sum with c[k]+incoming carry. Add up to eight products a[i]*b[k-i] before div/mod B; accumulate quotient in next-diagonal carry; retain remainder in sum. At diagonal end emit sum mod B and carry floor(sum/B); drain final carry. Exact identity follows from quotient/remainder and distributivity at each power B^k. With bounded operand lengths <=3000 limbs and B=10^9, incoming carry <=3001*B by induction; sum <8*(B-1)^2+3002*B < PHP_INT_MAX (64-bit). Reduces division/remainder count per diagonal from d to at most ceil(d/8)+1; no reduction in limb multiplication count, no asymptotic claim.
