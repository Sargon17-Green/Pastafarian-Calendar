# Stage49 PATCH24 — scalable legal weaving exact unrank (not yet closure)

For a given prefix, months 1..b have closed, b+1..a are active, and a+1..m are unopened. Active remaining letters have increasing last-occurrence order, giving `B=product C(runningSum-1,remaining-1)` exact possibilities. For unopened legal words, let D[k] count words whose first unopened month's final occurrence is at position k. Reversing both the word and month IDs converts this to the 1D newest-first distribution. Any active suffix word of S days can interleave its S letters before that final occurrence in `C(S+k-1,S)` ways, giving exact `B*sum D[k]*C(S+k-1,S)`. This is an exact completion count for each lexicographic candidate, without enumerating the vector-state DP.
