## Arithmetic and resource audit
Computed conservative bounds (exact BigInt arithmetic in audit): G<=137, C(136,16)=261429178502421685800 (68 bits), 17! 49 bits, 47! 198 bits, 120^47 325 bits, 47^5778 32095 bits/4012 bytes. Wide ring M^k for the last bound requires at most k=253, 4017 bytes. These are upper bounds, not actual counts or an allocation proof; wide intermediate carry/quotient/storage also need bounds. A 16-bit count cannot handle even S21/T22; 127-bit short selection cannot handle worst-case month name counts. Exact section N: use minimal k for M^k>=N, digits consecutive A_j-1 from same directional stream, A0 least significant, W=1+sum digits*M^j. For any ring size R=M or M^k, L=floor(R/N)*N; if initial W>L, forward first accepted is 1 after wrap, backward first accepted L; else use W. Rank=1+((accepted-1) mod N). This direct rejection skip is algebraically equivalent to unit steps, NOT independent redraw/uniform rejection. Host oracle must test explicit steps on small synthetic rings, including unreachable ranks, radix order, both directions, wrap, N=1,M,M+1 and M^k boundaries, count/rank full-width. Prove exact bounded arithmetic and bank/lease layout, including simultaneous operands and stack, before Native work. Single upper-bound operand fits 8192-byte bank but several may not; no fit claim.
